# Demo script — Field Shift, Feni (about 4 minutes)

Every number below is on screen in the app and traced in the docs named in
brackets. Say "satellite" and "rule", never "survey" or "ground truth".

## 1. The farmer (20 s)

A farmer in Feni Sadar grows Boro – Fallow – T. Aman, the commonest pattern
on this land. The question: keep it, adjust it, or shift it?

## 2. What the climate does to these crops (60 s)

Open "What your climate does to your crops".

* "28 years of NASA satellite rain is a short record. No clear trend does not
  mean no change." The limit is stated on screen first.
* T. Aman was hit by a flood-size downpour (408 mm or more in 7 days, a
  once-in-5-years amount) in **4 of the last 28 years**: 2004, 2017, 2019 and
  2024.
* 2024: **550 mm in 7 days**, the largest in 28 years; on the fitted curve,
  about once in 56 years. Point at the orange dot on the return-period chart.
* Boro had 35 °C or more in its flowering week in 16 of 27 years (temperature
  corrected to the BMD Feni station; caveat on screen).
  [docs/methodology/climate-shift.md §8]

## 3. The 2024 flood, seen from space (40 s)

Fulgazi demo field, 29 m from the Muhuri: green crop on 29 July → radar shows
open water on most passes from 4 August to 14 September (41 days) → green
again on 22 October, 79 days after the water came. One 90 m field, chosen by
rule.

## 4. Salinity, visible from orbit (40 s)

Open "What satellites saw on six Feni fields".

* **Coastal Sonagazi: the field lay fallow in 9 of 10 dry seasons. The five
  inland fields: fallow in 0 of the 32 dry seasons the satellites could read.**
* Say it carefully: the satellites see the empty winter field, not the salt.
  A fallow dry season on the coast is what soil salinity causes; no salinity
  was measured here.
  [docs/methodology/phenology.md]

## 5. What to do about it (40 s)

* Patterns are real Feni sequences, calendar-checked (Shahidullah et al. 2017;
  DAE BAMIS calendar).
* Under each hazard, the app lists the recommended patterns whose crops were
  hit in fewer years, with trade-offs labelled. Example: the only pattern with
  less flood exposure than Boro – Fallow – T. Aman drops Aman, and it says so.

## 6. Why you can trust the numbers (20 s)

* Rain from GPM IMERG, checked against the BMD Feni gauge (r = 0.95 year to
  year; GPM reads about 13 % low).
* BBS Feni Aman area fell from 66,130 ha to 26,753 ha in 2024-25. We checked
  the units in three Yearbook editions and DAE's own figure; we document an
  unexplained 0.03 % coincidence rather than hide it.
  [docs/methodology/bbs-aman-area-check.md]
